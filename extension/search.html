Search & Fetch by Title

📄 Batch download from sheet →

Upload a spreadsheet with just a title column. For each title, this opens 5 Chrome tabs that Google-search it, ranks the non-sponsored organic results by title match, and restricts them to the brand's official site or Amazon when one is found (falling back to other results otherwise). It visits candidates in order until one yields a product image (checked via structured Product data, then og:image, then the largest image on the page — generic logos/placeholders are filtered out) — then zips everything up.

⚠️
This automates real Google searches in visible tabs — it can trip Google's bot detection (a CAPTCHA in one of the tabs) on large sheets. If that happens, solve it in that tab and the search will keep going, or cancel and re-run the remaining rows. Small randomized delays are added between searches to reduce this.
